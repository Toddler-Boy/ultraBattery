# Changelog

## 1.0.0 (2026-10-08)

First release.

- Tray icon per connected Bluetooth keyboard, mouse and gamepad, showing the battery percentage and a device symbol
- Tooltip with device name and battery level
- Connects and disconnects show up within seconds, battery levels refresh every 60 seconds
- Orange at 30% and below, red at 15% and below
- Light and dark taskbar themes, display scaling changes
- Start with Windows, switchable from the right-click menu
- Bluetooth adapters that are plugged in or re-enumerated after sleep are picked up automatically
